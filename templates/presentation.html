Pillar 1: Problem Definition

The Challenge: $100B+ Lost Annually to Retail Shrinkage

Traditional CCTV cameras record crimes—they do not prevent them.

💸
Economic Shrinkage
  • Over $100 Billion lost every year to retail theft, shoplifting, and distraction-theft globally.
  • Incidents happen in mere seconds; human guards cannot be everywhere.
  • Post-incident review is too late—merchandise and suspects are already long gone.
👁️
Human Visual Fatigue
  • Security operators monitor 15 to 40 video feeds simultaneously.
  • Vigilance drops by over 50% within just 20 minutes of continuous monitoring.
  • Critical theft moments are missed due to natural human fatigue and blind spots.
🏬
Who It Affects
  • Retail Owners & Chains: Destroys profit margins and causes inventory write-offs.
  • Loss Prevention Teams: Exhausted by endless manual forensic footage scouring.
  • Store Staff: Face safety risks during unexpected theft confrontations.
Pillar 2: The Solution

Anti-Theft Tracker: Real-Time Intelligent Triage

Turning passive video cameras into active, automated sentinels.

⚡
Proactive Threat Detection
  • Runs high-speed YOLOv8 deep learning at 30+ FPS directly on video feeds.
  • Tracks individuals, body movements, and high-value target merchandise (purses, phones, bags).
  • Evaluates suspicious inter-personal closeness and distraction-theft interactions.
📸
Evidence Photo Grid
  • Automatically snaps crime scene photos the instant theft occurs.
  • Displays images in a full-width gallery below the video screen.
  • Interactive Lightbox Modal: Inspect high-res frames with 1-click download.
👥
Audience It Serves
  • Store Managers: Instant floor alerts and telemetry incident audit logs.
  • Security Guards: Actionable triage—intervene while theft is taking place.
  • System Integrators: Plug-and-play FastAPI microservice for NVR / IP cameras.

Pillar 4: Innovation & Technology

Deep Learning Meets Spatial Interaction Heuristics

Engineered for low-latency inference, real-time streaming, and forensic accuracy.

🧠
YOLOv8 Neural Core
  • High-throughput bounding box detection recognizing persons and merchandise.
  • Supports fine-tuned custom theft weights or standard COCO classes.
  • Automatic device adaptation: runs smoothly on CUDA GPU or standard CPU.
📐
Spatial Proximity Engine
  • O(n²) multi-person proximity and bounding-box interaction analytics.
  • Evaluates 2D body overlap, approach speeds, and dwell time in critical zones.
  • Differentiates normal customer presence from suspicious pickpocketing and theft.
🌐
Asynchronous Web Stack
  • FastAPI asynchronous streaming delivering low-latency MJPEG feeds.
  • Disk-backed persistent snapshot storage (GET /api/snapshots) across page reloads.
  • Dynamic Head-Up Display (HUD) with colored bounding boxes and FPS telemetry.
